THE FREE AI HARBOR WORKSPACE

Use it here.
Make it yours.

Free AI Harbor has two ways to create: on-device models with no account, and cloud providers with free API access or credits. Open a tool to see its allowance, add your input, and run it right here.

First use

The selected AI model and runtime files download from their public hosts. Downloads range from a few megabytes to several hundred. Your browser may cache them, but clearing its storage or cache can require another download.

Your device

On-device tools process inputs locally. A recent desktop browser with several gigabytes of free memory works best. Older phones or browsers may run slowly or fail to load a model.

What’s included

123 on-device workflows and 27 connected workflows across writing, research, marketing, learning, business, images, audio, and coding. These are task workflows, not 150 distinct AI models. Writing tools share a small local language model; specialist tools use embeddings, classifiers, OCR, and speech models. Quality varies by task. Use short, clear inputs and review the output.

Your information

AI Chat conversations and saved-tool bookmarks are stored in this browser. Other tool inputs and results stay in this tab. Clearing browser storage removes saved chats. On-device inputs are not sent to an AI service. For connected tools, pressing Run sends your input and key directly from your browser to the selected provider. Provider keys and cloud conversations are kept only in this tab’s memory and cleared by reloading. Providers apply their own retention, usage, and billing terms.

Free credits & top-ups

Connected tools require your own provider account and API key. The linked allowance was reviewed on 12 October 2026 and can change. Monthly credits reset; one-time trial credits do not. Shared provider balances can include paid credits, so check the provider dashboard before running. Free AI Harbor does not sell credits, pool allowances, or switch your plan. Some free plans restrict commercial use; each tool shows the relevant limitation.

AI models, software, and licenses

Runtime: Hugging Face Transformers.js 3.8.1 and Tesseract.js 6.0.1. Model names and licenses below are supplied by their publishers. German translation uses Helsinki-NLP Opus MT under CC BY 4.0. MobileNet uses the publisher’s model terms.
