How Free AI Harbor works

Free AI Harbor has two ways to create: on-device models with no account, and cloud providers with free API access or credits. Open a tool to see its allowance, add your input, and run it right here.

On-device tools

Your text, images, and audio stay in your browser. Models download once and run locally. No account is required.

Connected tools

These send your input to an external provider using an API key you provide. Check your provider’s free quota or credits before running. Free AI Harbor does not store your keys.

Free credits & top-ups

Connected tools require your own provider account and API key. The linked allowance was reviewed on 12 October 2026 and can change. Monthly credits reset; one-time trial credits do not. Shared provider balances can include paid credits, so check the provider dashboard before running. Free AI Harbor does not sell credits, pool allowances, or switch your plan. Some free plans restrict commercial use; each tool shows the relevant limitation.
