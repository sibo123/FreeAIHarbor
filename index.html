Choose the right workflow before you start

Free AI Harbor offers 131 local workflows and 27 optional connected workflows. The collection includes AI models, reusable writing prompts, and browser utilities. Several tools share a model; they are not 158 separate AI systems.

Local AI and utilities

Local AI downloads a model before processing your input on your device. The Photo Editor, Logo Designer, and AI Prompt Builder use filters or templates without an AI model. A recent desktop browser works best for larger models.

Connected provider tools

These tools send your input to an external provider using your API key. Check your account allowance and billing first: a free tier or trial does not mean unlimited free use. Your provider may charge after its allowance is exhausted.
