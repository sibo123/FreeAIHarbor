Common questions

A tool isn't loading for me

On-device tools download AI model files on first use (a few MB to several hundred MB). Check your internet connection and try a recent desktop browser like Chrome or Edge. Mobile browsers may struggle with larger models.

Is my data safe when I use a tool?

Yes. On-device tools process everything locally — nothing is uploaded to our servers. For cloud tools, inputs go directly from your browser to the provider. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I suggest a new AI tool?

Absolutely! Use the form on this page, select "Tool suggestion" as the subject, and describe the tool you'd like to see. We review all suggestions.

How do I report a bug?

Select "Bug report" in the form, tell us which tool is affected, what you were trying to do, and what happened instead. Screenshots are very helpful.
