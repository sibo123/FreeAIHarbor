Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices — including how we handle uploaded files and advertising.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who We Are

Free AI Harbor (freeaiharbor.com) is a free, browser-based AI tools workspace. We provide 150+ on-device and cloud-connected AI workflows that run directly in your browser. "We", "us", and "our" refer to the operators of Free AI Harbor.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Data

When you visit Free AI Harbor, our hosting provider (Hostinger) and analytics services may automatically collect standard server log information, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL and pages visited
  • Date and time of access

2.2 Data You Provide

A Free AI Harbor account is not required. If you contact us, we receive the name, email address, subject, and message you submit. The contact form sends these fields to Formspree for delivery. Tool inputs are handled separately as described in Section 5. Do not include API keys or sensitive documents in support messages.

2.3 Browser Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to store:

  • Your saved tool bookmarks
  • AI Chat conversation history (on-device chats only)
  • Your tool preferences and settings

This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings.

3. Cookies

Saved chats and bookmarks use browser storage rather than advertising cookies. Hosting services and any enabled analytics or advertising services may use cookies under their own settings and policies. Cookie names and retention periods vary; we do not claim a fixed two-year lifetime for every service.

You can remove browser storage through your browser settings. If advertising consent controls are displayed, use them to manage your advertising choices. Google also provides ad personalization settings.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

If Google AdSense advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. Advertising availability and consent options depend on the configuration and visitor region.

Key points:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to Free AI Harbor and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • You may also opt out via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.
  • For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

We do not control the cookies placed by third-party advertisers. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. How Uploaded Images & Files Are Processed

Many Free AI Harbor tools accept image, audio, or text file uploads (for example, Image to Text, Audio Transcriber, Portrait Background Remover).

Processing depends on the tool you choose. Local tools process files in your browser. Connected tools send your input or uploaded file to the selected external provider when you run them.
  • On-device tools: Your files are read into browser memory only. They are not uploaded to any external server. Closing or reloading the tab clears temporary file inputs. Saved chats and bookmarks remain in browser storage until deleted.
  • Cloud-connected tools (e.g. Gemini, Groq): If you use a cloud-connected tool and provide an API key, your input (which may include text or images) is sent directly from your browser to the selected third-party provider (e.g. Google, Groq). Free AI Harbor does not relay, store, or log this data. The provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle it.
  • Retention: We do not retain uploaded files. No file data is written to our servers at any point.

6. How We Use Your Data

We use the automatically collected data described in Section 2.1 to:

  • Operate and improve the service
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns (anonymised)
  • Display contextually relevant advertisements via Google AdSense

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

7. Data Sharing

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Our hosting provider (Hostinger) processes server logs as necessary to operate the site. They are bound by their own data processing terms.
  • Contact delivery: Formspree processes contact form submissions. See Formspree’s privacy policy.
  • Model hosts: Loading local AI models and OCR files connects to their public hosts. Those hosts receive network metadata such as an IP address, but local tool inputs are not included in the download.
  • Connected AI providers: The selected provider receives your API request and input under its own privacy terms.
  • Advertising partners: When advertising is enabled, Google may receive data as described in Section 4.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Opt out of personalised advertising (see Section 4)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

You can remove saved chats and bookmarks through browser settings. For information submitted through the contact form, contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. To exercise any rights or ask questions, contact us at the address in Section 10.

9. Children's Privacy

Free AI Harbor is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us via our Contact page.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after any change constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
