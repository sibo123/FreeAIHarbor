How the Tools Work

On-Device Tools (131 tools)

Most of our tools run entirely on your device using WebAssembly. AI models are downloaded from their public hosts (Hugging Face) directly to your browser on first use. After that, your browser may cache them so subsequent runs are faster.

Your inputs are never sent to our servers. Everything is processed locally in your browser tab. Closing the tab clears all data.

Cloud-Connected Tools (27 tools)

Some tools — such as Gemini Assistant, Groq Fast Assistant, and Mistral Writer — connect to third-party AI providers. These tools require a free API key from the relevant provider (Google AI Studio, Groq, etc.). When you run such a tool, your input is sent directly from your browser to the provider. Free AI Harbor does not see, store, or relay this data. Provider API keys are held only in your browser's session memory and are cleared when you close or reload the tab.

Who Runs Free AI Harbor?

Free AI Harbor is an independent project dedicated to making practical AI tools freely available on the web. We are not affiliated with any AI model provider. We do not develop or train the underlying AI models — we integrate and present best-in-class open-source and free-tier models inside a clean, accessible workspace.

The site is maintained by its founders and a small team of contributors. Our goal is to continuously expand the tool library, improve the user experience, and keep everything free.

How We Stay Free

Free AI Harbor is supported by Google AdSense advertisements. Ads are displayed on the site to cover operating costs (hosting, maintenance, development). We do not sell your data. We do not show intrusive pop-ups. Ads are clearly labelled and placed so they do not interrupt your workflow.

If you find the tools useful, simply using the site — and occasionally interacting with relevant ads — helps keep Free AI Harbor free for everyone.
