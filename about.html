About Free AI Harbor

One place for every free AI tool you actually need — no account, no credit card, no catch.

Our Mission

Free AI Harbor exists because powerful AI should not be locked behind expensive subscriptions or complex developer setups. We believe anyone — a student writing an essay, a freelancer polishing a proposal, a small business owner creating content — should have access to state-of-the-art AI tools right in their browser.

The workspace contains 158 task workflows: 131 run on your device and 27 connect to an external provider. Local workflows have no usage charge from Free AI Harbor. Connected workflows require your own provider account and API key; provider quotas, trial eligibility, commercial-use restrictions, and charges may apply.

What Is Free AI Harbor?

Free AI Harbor is a browser-based AI workspace. Local tools need no Free AI Harbor account or subscription. AI models download on first use and need sufficient browser memory. Connected tools require a separate provider account and may incur provider charges.

How the Tools Work

On-Device Tools (131 tools)

Most of our tools run entirely on your device using WebAssembly. AI models are downloaded from their public hosts (Hugging Face) directly to your browser on first use. After that, your browser may cache them so subsequent runs are faster.

Your inputs are never sent to our servers. Local tool inputs are processed in your browser tab. Saved chat history and bookmarks persist in browser storage until you delete them; model files may remain cached.

Cloud-Connected Tools (27 tools)

Some tools — such as Gemini Assistant, Groq Fast Assistant, and Mistral Assistant — connect to third-party AI providers. These tools require an API key from the relevant provider (Google AI Studio, Groq, etc.). When you run such a tool, your input is sent directly from your browser to the provider. Free AI Harbor does not see, store, or relay this data. Provider API keys are held only in your browser's session memory and are cleared when you close or reload the tab.

Who Runs Free AI Harbor?

Free AI Harbor is an independent project dedicated to making practical AI tools freely available on the web. We are not affiliated with any AI model provider. We do not develop or train the underlying AI models — we integrate and present best-in-class open-source and free-tier models inside a clean, accessible workspace.

Free AI Harbor is maintained by Haseeb Shah, a video editor and graphic designer. The workspace brings together practical writing, media, and research workflows. Several workflows share the same underlying model; the tool count is not a count of separate AI models.

How We Stay Free

Free AI Harbor does not charge a subscription for access to this workspace. Advertising may support hosting and maintenance if enabled. External AI providers manage their own accounts, prices, and billing separately.
