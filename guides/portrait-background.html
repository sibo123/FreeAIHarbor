Remove a portrait background and keep a usable PNG

Choose a suitable portrait, understand output size, and check edges before using your cutout.

Start with a photograph of a person

The Portrait Background Remover uses MODNet, a model designed for people. Choose a well-lit photograph with a clear outline. A wallet, shoe, or bottle is a different task; this portrait tool is not a reliable general product cutout service.

Accepted files are JPG, PNG, WebP, and BMP under 15 MB. Before inference, the workspace reduces images whose longest edge exceeds 1600 pixels. For example, a 4000 × 3000 upload is processed at 1600 × 1200. Keep your original file if you need a larger print version.

From upload to download

  1. Open the tool and choose your portrait. Check the preview to confirm you selected the right image.
  2. Choose Remove background. First use downloads approximately 26 MB of model files plus runtime files. Keep the tab open until it finishes.
  3. Compare the original with the result. Look at hair, fingers, shoulders, and any pale clothing near a bright background.
  4. Choose Download PNG. PNG preserves transparency; converting the cutout to JPEG removes the alpha channel.

Check the edge, not just the face

Place the downloaded PNG over both a dark and a light background in your design editor. A pale halo that disappears on white may show against navy. If fine hair has vanished, use a clearer original or touch up the mask manually. Running the same image repeatedly does not guarantee a better edge.

The model estimates foreground pixels; it does not rebuild missing body parts or add a new background. For a team page, leave enough space around the shoulders and use a consistent crop across portraits.

If processing fails

Try a smaller image, close memory-heavy tabs, and check that model downloads are not blocked. Use Stop to cancel a local run. A download error is not evidence that your photograph is unsuitable; it may be a connection or storage problem.
